How we roll it out

Low risk, low cost, measurable at every step.

1

Pilot (4 weeks)

One brand, 2–3 branches. Upload existing SOPs. Crew get the assistant on WhatsApp or their phone browser. Measure: questions answered, quiz scores, time saved.

2

Learn & adjust

See which questions get asked most — that tells us exactly where the SOPs are unclear. Supervisors save answering time; we measure it.

3

Scale group-wide

Roll out to all brands and branches, connect the manager dashboard to live data, add spaced-repetition micro-quizzes for certifications.

4

Advanced (phase 2)

AI role-play practice (handling angry customers), auto-generated quizzes from SOP updates, and integration with the existing Cognify learning platform.
